Editorial / Swiss Tech

A radical departure: minimal, high-contrast, off-white bone paper aesthetic with monumental Syne typography, strict 1px hairline Swiss grid lines, and interactive floating cursor hover previews.

Restraint as high-craft. Eliminates all decorative blobs; creativity stems entirely from layout, scale, rhythm, and typography.
